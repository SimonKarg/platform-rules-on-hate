          1 Named is not the same as protected. A group can stay on a platform's list while the rules that spell out what protecting it means are removed or qualified: X still names gender identity, but since April 2023 no longer says that misgendering or deadnaming a transgender person is an attack; Meta still names sexual orientation and gender identity, but since January 2025 allows allegations of mental illness or abnormality based on them. Reading the full rules, not just the list, is what shows how far a group is protected. And what a platform actually enforces is a separate question that its written rules cannot answer. ↩
